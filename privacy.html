Privacy Policy

Summary

SkyCheck has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We collect only what's needed to run your account and subscription, and we don't sell or share your personal information. Effective September 28, 2026.

Your account

When you create an account we store your email address, a user ID, the sign-in method you used (Apple, Google or email), and your name if Apple or Google shares it. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address instead. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Accounts are hosted by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider.

Your flight log

Log entries include the date, duration, drone, location name and coordinates, client, notes, and the forecast conditions at the time.

LAANC requests

When you request a LAANC authorization, the flight area, altitude, times, flight type and name you enter are sent to our LAANC provider (currently Airspace Link) and stored with your account so you can see their status. You enter your pilot details, such as name, phone and certificate number, on the provider's own page; they're handled under the provider's privacy policy and sent to the FAA as LAANC requires.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's Apple push token with your account so our server can alert you, for example when someone replies to you or messages you, when a group flight you're going to changes or is about to start, when the weather at a place you watch goes outside your limits, or when a LAANC request changes status. For Lock Screen cards (Live Activities), such as a group flight or a LAANC authorization, we store that card's update token until the card ends. Signing out removes the tokens for that device. The civil twilight reminder and flying-window reminders are scheduled on your device and don't use our server.

Subscriptions

Purchases are made through Apple; we never see your payment details. When you subscribe, the purchase is linked to your SkyCheck user ID, and Apple notifies our server of renewals, cancellations and refunds. We store your plan, its status, expiry date, auto-renew setting and Apple's transaction ID with your account, so Pro works wherever you sign in.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings › Account. This deletes your account record and your synced drones, spots and log from our servers and, for Sign in with Apple, revokes SkyCheck's access to your Apple ID. It doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.

Marketplace

If you use the marketplace, we store the name you choose to show other members, your listings (title, description, price, condition, photos, and an approximate location rounded to about 1 km), listings you save, people you block, reports you make, and your messages with other members. Listings and your public name, profile photo and bio are visible to other signed-in members. Messages are visible only to you and the other member, except that we may review messages that are reported, to enforce the Terms of Use. Location data is removed from photos before they're uploaded. When you delete a listing, its photos and chats are deleted; when you delete your account, all of your marketplace data is deleted.

Community

If you use the community, we store your posts, comments, photos, votes, reports you make and people you block. Posts, comments, photos, your public name and karma are visible to other signed-in members; your votes aren't. Location data is removed from photos before they're uploaded, and anything you attach from SkyCheck (a flight, spot or conditions) shows its place rounded to about 1 km. When someone replies to you, our server sends you a notification. If you post a group flight, its meeting place and time are visible to other members. When you say you're going to a group flight, other members can see that, and when you check in we store the time you checked in, not your location. We also store who you follow. Deleting a post or comment removes its text and photos; deleting your account deletes all of your community data.

Your location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show the forecast and airspace where you are. To get a forecast, the app sends approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) to our weather provider, Open-Meteo, or to our server, which passes them on to Open-Meteo. If you turn on weather change alerts, watch a saved spot, or have an approved LAANC authorization with LAANC alerts on, our server checks the weather every 10 minutes for your chosen forecast place (already saved with your settings), the watched spot, or the authorization's area, sending rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo. For measured wind at the nearest airport, it sends an area of about 130 km around the forecast place to NOAA's Aviation Weather Center. To show radar, it requests radar images for the map area around the forecast place from NOAA. To show airspace, it sends the map area on screen to the FAA's map service or to our server. We don't store your location on our servers or link it to you.

Search and maps

Place searches, address lookups and maps are handled by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

Data saved to your account

Your drone profiles (drone model, limits, how you fly, and their order), saved spots (name, coordinates, order and favorites), flight log, and settings (units, the drone you're flying today, the forecast place you picked, flying-window and alert options, how your log is sorted, map layers and style, and the starting values for new LAANC requests) are saved to your account so they sync across your devices. Only you can read them. They're deleted when you delete them or your account. When you sign out, they're removed from that device but stay in your account.

Data stored on your device

Your device's notification permission, its current location, and cached forecast, satellite and airspace data are stored only on your device. When the forecast is set to Current location, your position isn't saved to your account. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Service providers

Like any internet service, the providers the app connects to (Supabase, Google if you sign in with Google, Open-Meteo, NOAA, CelesTrak, the FAA and Esri, Apple, and our server host) can see your device's IP address and the request made. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

SkyCheck isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it in the app and change the effective date.
