Support

Questions, problems or feedback about SkyCheck: email support@gocrewhand.app.

Pro isn't showing after I subscribed

Open Settings › Account in SkyCheck and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same SkyCheck account and Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel or change my subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then SkyCheck. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

Delete my account

In SkyCheck, open Settings › Account and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and the drones, spots, log, marketplace and community data saved with it.

I'm not getting notifications

In SkyCheck, open Settings › Alerts & widgets and check that notifications are on for this device. If they're off, open the Settings app › Notifications › SkyCheck and turn on Allow Notifications.

The forecast says the weather service is busy

SkyCheck tries again automatically in a few minutes, or tap Try again. Your drone's limits and saved spots aren't affected.

Report a post, listing or member

Tap the ••• menu on the post, listing, message or profile and choose Report. We review reports within 24 hours.

Safety

SkyCheck shows weather forecasts, space-weather and satellite estimates, airspace information and limits based on published drone specifications. It is for general information and planning only. It is not an official aviation weather briefing, not an FAA product, and not an authorization to fly anywhere. Verdicts such as "Within your limits" only compare forecast data with the limits you chose; they are not a statement that a flight is safe or legal.
